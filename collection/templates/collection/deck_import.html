What can I paste?

The text export from Moxfield, Archidekt, MTG Arena, MTGO, EDHREC or TappedOut. One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring (CMM) 410, 4x Lightning Bolt or just Counterspell.

  • Section headers decide where cards go: Commander, Deck or Main, Sideboard, Companion (goes in the sideboard). Maybeboard and Considering sections are skipped.
  • Tags work too: *CMDR* (Moxfield), [Commander] or [Sideboard] (Archidekt), and SB: at the start of a line.
  • With no headers, everything goes in the main deck. You can move a card to another section on the deck page afterwards.
